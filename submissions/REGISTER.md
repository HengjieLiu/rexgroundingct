## Naming and postprocessing

- **1 — Raw:** Thresholded prediction without anatomy postprocessing. For d1 the threshold is applied after the probability average.
- **2 — Postprocess 2:** Eligible whole-lung support (labels 10–14), physically dilated 20 mm, applied to the raw variant; ineligible findings remain unchanged.
- **3 — Postprocess 3:** Eligible prompt-selected lung side/lobe support, physically dilated 20 mm, applied independently to the raw variant; ineligible or unassigned routes remain unchanged.
- **11 — Collaborator semantic-v1:** d11 = d1 intersected with frozen semantic-v1 support; preserves collaborator parser, whole-lung fallback and absence of our category exclusions.
- **12 — Collaborator semantic-v2 strict:** d12 = d11 followed by the five frozen hard-eligible semantic-v2 signatures. Unselected findings retain d11.

c1/c2/c3 are aliases for baseline/whole_lung20/fine20; existing folders retain their names.
