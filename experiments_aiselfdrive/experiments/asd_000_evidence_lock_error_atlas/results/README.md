# Results

Revision-2 scientific outputs and distinct completion-evidence files live
here. Names begin with `revision_2_`; completion evidence is never also a stage
output, avoiding self-referential hashes. `result.json` is the controller-owned
live projection and may be replaced after every stage; other unprefixed files
are revision-1 historical material and are not revision-2 GO evidence. Heavy
logits stay in the external runtime and are referenced by a hashed source
manifest.
